Piholegotchi

Hello. I’m Muncher.

I eat the ads your network blocks and keep an eye on your day from a window on your smaller screen. Connect what you use; anything you skip stays hidden.

Coming from the Pi?

On the Pi’s settings page, go to Device › Download backup, then load that file here. AdGuard, weather, calendar, Home Assistant, bins and reminders come across. Spotify connects fresh, so the Pi stays signed in too.

What’s connected

Updates

New versions come from GitHub. Muncher checks every few hours and says when one’s ready.

Muncher

His window

Muncher is a normal window: drag it to your smaller screen, resize it, and he’ll open there next time. The tray icon shows or hides him. Stroke him to say hello, and drop a link on him to block that site.

Moods

In his window

Untick anything you don’t want. Things that aren’t connected hide by themselves, and a tab with nothing in it disappears.

Ads and music

Spotify

Use the same Spotify app as the Pi, or make one at developer.spotify.com. Its redirect URI must be http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback.

Play, pause and skip need Spotify Premium.
